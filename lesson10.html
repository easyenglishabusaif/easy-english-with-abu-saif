🔟 Past Perfect Continuous

الماضي التام المستمر

📚 What is Past Perfect Continuous?

نستخدم الماضي التام المستمر للتحدث عن فعل كان مستمرًا لفترة من الزمن قبل حدوث شيء آخر في الماضي.

We use the Past Perfect Continuous to talk about an action that had been continuing for some time before another action in the past.

💡 Basic Rule

Subject + had + been + verb-ing

جميع الضمائر تستخدم: had been

I / You / He / She / It / We / They → had been

✍️ Examples

I had been studying for two hours before I went to bed.

She had been working all day before she went home.

He had been playing football for an hour before it started raining.

We had been waiting for thirty minutes before the bus arrived.

Abu Saif had been studying English for three hours before he took the test.

⏰ For & Since

نستخدم for للحديث عن مدة زمنية.

for two hours

for three days

for five years


نستخدم since لتحديد بداية الفترة الزمنية.

since Monday

since 2020

since morning

⏳ Remember

Past Perfect Continuous يركز على استمرار الفعل ومدة حدوثه قبل حدث آخر في الماضي.

She had been studying for two hours before she went to sleep.

أولًا: كانت تدرس لمدة ساعتين.

ثانيًا: ذهبت للنوم.

❌ Negative

نستخدم: had not been + verb-ing

had not been = hadn't been

I hadn't been sleeping well.

She hadn't been working for very long.

They hadn't been waiting for an hour.

He hadn't been feeling well.

❓ Questions

Had you been studying before the test?

Had she been working all day?

Had they been waiting for a long time?

Had he been playing football?

💬 Short Answers

Yes, I had.

No, I hadn't.

Yes, she had.

No, she hadn't.

⭐ Important Words

for = لمدة

I had been studying for two hours.

since = منذ

She had been working since morning.

before = قبل

They had been waiting before the bus arrived.

when = عندما

When I arrived, he had been sleeping.

🔄 Past Perfect vs Past Perfect Continuous

Past Perfect يركز على اكتمال الفعل قبل حدث آخر.

She had finished her homework before dinner.

أما Past Perfect Continuous فيركز على مدة استمرار الفعل قبل حدث آخر.

She had been studying for two hours before dinner.

🔄 Present vs Past

Present Perfect Continuous

I have been studying for two hours.

الفعل مرتبط بالحاضر.


Past Perfect Continuous

I had been studying for two hours before dinner.

الفعل حدث قبل حدث آخر في الماضي.
