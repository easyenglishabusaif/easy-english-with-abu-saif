2️⃣ Verb to Be

الفعل to be في المضارع له ثلاث صور: am / is / are

Verb Pronoun Example المعنى
am I I am a student. أنا طالب.
is He He is happy. هو سعيد.
is She She is a teacher. هي معلمة.
is It It is a cat. إنها قطة.
are You You are happy. أنت سعيد.
are We We are friends. نحن أصدقاء.
are They They are students. هم طلاب.

💡 Easy Rule

I → am

He / She / It → is

You / We / They → are

✍️ Examples

I am Abu Saif.

She is a teacher.

He is my brother.

We are friends.

They are students.

❌ Negative

I am not a student.

He is not tired.

She is not a doctor.

They are not ready.

❓ Questions

Am I late?

Is he a teacher?

Is she happy?

Are they students?
