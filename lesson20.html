1️⃣ Pronouns

I = أنا

You = أنت / أنتم

He = هو

She = هي

It = لغير العاقل

We = نحن

They = هم / هن

I am Abu Saif.

2️⃣ Verb to Be

I → am

He / She / It → is

You / We / They → are

She is happy.

3️⃣ Present Simple

نستخدمه للعادات والأشياء المتكررة والحقائق.

We use it for habits, routines and facts.

I work every day.

4️⃣ Present Continuous

نستخدمه للأشياء التي تحدث الآن.

We use it for actions happening now.

They are playing football.

5️⃣ Past Simple

نستخدمه للأشياء التي حدثت في الماضي.

We use it for completed past actions.

I visited London yesterday.

6️⃣ Past Continuous

شيء كان يحدث في وقت معين في الماضي.

I was watching TV at 8 PM.

7️⃣ 8️⃣ 9️⃣ 🔟 Perfect Tenses

Present Perfect = have / has + past participle

I have finished my work.

Past Perfect = had + past participle

She had left before I arrived.

Present Perfect Continuous = have / has been + verb-ing

I have been studying for two hours.

Past Perfect Continuous = had been + verb-ing

He had been working for three hours.

1️⃣1️⃣ Future Simple

نستخدم will للتحدث عن المستقبل.

I will call you tomorrow.

1️⃣2️⃣ Modal Verbs

can = يستطيع

could = استطاع / يمكن

should = ينبغي

must = يجب

You should study English.

1️⃣3️⃣ Adverbs

تصف كيف أو متى أو كم مرة يحدث الفعل.

slowly, quickly, always, often

He runs quickly.

1️⃣4️⃣ Adjectives

تصف الأشخاص والأشياء والأماكن.

big, small, happy, beautiful

Abu Saif is happy.

1️⃣5️⃣ Prepositions

in = في

on = على

at = عند / في

The book is on the table.

1️⃣6️⃣ Questions

What = ماذا / ما

Where = أين

When = متى

Why = لماذا

Who = من

How = كيف

Where do you live?

1️⃣7️⃣ Comparatives & Superlatives

tall → taller → the tallest

small → smaller → the smallest

Ali is taller than Omar.

1️⃣8️⃣ Linking Words

and = و

but = لكن

because = لأن

so = لذلك

or = أو

I was tired, so I went home.

1️⃣9️⃣ Passive Voice

نستخدم المبني للمجهول عندما يكون التركيز على الفعل أو الشيء الذي وقع عليه الفعل.

The cake was made yesterday.

2️⃣0️⃣ Reported Speech

نستخدم الكلام المنقول لنخبر شخصًا بما قاله شخص آخر.

Direct: "I am happy."

Reported: He said that he was happy.
