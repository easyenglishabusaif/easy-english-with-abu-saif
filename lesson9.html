9️⃣ Present Perfect Continuous

المضارع التام المستمر

📚 What is Present Perfect Continuous?

نستخدم المضارع التام المستمر للتحدث عن فعل بدأ في الماضي وما زال مستمرًا حتى الآن، أو انتهى منذ وقت قريب وله أثر واضح الآن.

We use the Present Perfect Continuous for an action that started in the past and is still continuing now, or has recently stopped.

💡 Basic Rule

Subject + have / has + been + verb-ing

I / You / We / They → have been

He / She / It → has been

✍️ Examples

I have been studying English for two hours.

She has been working since morning.

He has been playing football for an hour.

We have been learning English since January.

They have been waiting for thirty minutes.

⏰ For & Since

نستخدم for للحديث عن مدة زمنية.

for two hours

for three days

for five years


نستخدم since لتحديد بداية الفترة الزمنية.

since Monday

since 2020

since morning

⭐ More Examples

I have been studying for two hours.

She has been working since 8 o'clock.

They have been living here for five years.

He has been playing football since morning.

❌ Negative

نستخدم: have not / has not + been + verb-ing

have not = haven't

has not = hasn't

I haven't been sleeping well.

She hasn't been feeling well.

They haven't been working today.

❓ Questions

Have you been studying English?

Has she been working all day?

Have they been waiting for you?

Has he been playing football?

💬 Short Answers

Yes, I have.

No, I haven't.

Yes, she has.

No, she hasn't.

⭐ Remember

بعد have / has نستخدم: been + verb-ing

I have been studying. ✅

She has been working. ✅

She has been work. ❌

🔄 Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

Present Perfect يركز غالبًا على النتيجة أو اكتمال الفعل.

I have finished my homework.

لقد أنهيت واجبي.


Present Perfect Continuous يركز على استمرار الفعل أو مدته.

I have been studying for two hours.

لقد كنت أدرس لمدة ساعتين.

🎯 When do we use it?

1️⃣ فعل بدأ في الماضي وما زال مستمرًا.

I have been learning English for two years.

2️⃣ فعل استمر لفترة وانتهى منذ وقت قريب وله أثر الآن.

She is tired because she has been working all day.
