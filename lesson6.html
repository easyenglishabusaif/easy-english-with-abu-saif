6️⃣ Past Continuous

الماضي المستمر

📚 What is Past Continuous?

We use the Past Continuous to talk about an action that was happening at a specific time in the past.

نستخدم الماضي المستمر للتحدث عن فعل كان مستمرًا في وقت معين في الماضي.

Example:

I was studying at 8 PM.

كنت أدرس الساعة الثامنة مساءً.

🧩 The Formula

Subject + was / were + verb-ing

I / He / She / It → was

You / We / They → were

💬 Examples

I was studying English.

He was sleeping.

She was cooking dinner.

We were watching TV.

They were playing football.

❌ Negative

We use: was not / were not

was not = wasn't

were not = weren't

I wasn't sleeping.

He wasn't working.

They weren't playing.

❓ Questions

Was he sleeping?

Was she cooking?

Were they playing football?

Were you studying?

Short Answers

Yes, he was.

No, she wasn't.

Yes, they were.

⭐ When & While

We often use when and while with the Past Continuous.

I was sleeping when my phone rang.

While I was studying, my brother was watching TV.

عندما كنت أدرس، كان أخي يشاهد التلفاز.

🔄 Past Simple vs Past Continuous

Past Simple: حدث وانتهى.

I watched TV yesterday.

Past Continuous: كان يحدث في وقت معين.

I was watching TV at 8 PM.

Example:

I was watching TV when my friend called.

⏰ Time Expressions

at 7 PM = الساعة السابعة مساءً

at that time = في ذلك الوقت

while = بينما

when = عندما
