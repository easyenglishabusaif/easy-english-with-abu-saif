8️⃣ Past Perfect

الماضي التام

📚 What is Past Perfect?

نستخدم الماضي التام للتحدث عن حدث وقع قبل حدث آخر في الماضي.

We use the Past Perfect for an action that happened before another action in the past.

💡 Basic Rule

Subject + had + V3

جميع الضمائر تستخدم had.

I / You / He / She / It / We / They → had

✍️ Examples

I had finished my homework before dinner.

She had left before I arrived.

He had eaten breakfast before he went to school.

We had studied English before the test.

They had cleaned the room before I called.

⏳ Remember

الحدث الأول: Past Perfect

الحدث الثاني: Past Simple

She had left before I arrived.

أولًا: غادرت هي.

ثانيًا: وصلت أنا.

⭐ What is V3?

V3 هو التصريف الثالث للفعل.

go → went → gone

eat → ate → eaten

see → saw → seen

finish → finished → finished

leave → left → left

❌ Negative

نستخدم: had not + V3

had not = hadn't

I hadn't finished my homework.

She hadn't left before I arrived.

They hadn't eaten dinner.

He hadn't seen the movie.

❓ Questions

Had you finished your homework?

Had she left before you arrived?

Had they eaten dinner?

Had he seen the movie?

💬 Short Answers

Yes, I had.

No, I hadn't.

Yes, she had.

No, she hadn't.

⭐ Important Words

before = قبل

She had left before I arrived.

after = بعد

After he had eaten, he went to work.

already = بالفعل

They had already left.

when = عندما

When I arrived, they had gone.

🔄 Past Simple vs Past Perfect

Past Simple يتحدث عن حدث وقع في الماضي.

Past Perfect يوضح أن حدثًا وقع قبل حدث آخر في الماضي.

When I arrived, she had left.

غادرت هي أولًا، ثم وصلت أنا.

She had left before I arrived. ✅
