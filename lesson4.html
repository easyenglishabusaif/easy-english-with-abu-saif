4️⃣ Present Continuous

المضارع المستمر

📚 What is Present Continuous?

We use the Present Continuous to talk about actions happening now.

نستخدم المضارع المستمر للتحدث عن الأشياء التي تحدث الآن.

🧩 The Formula

I + am + verb-ing

I am studying.

He / She / It + is + verb-ing

She is reading.

You / We / They + are + verb-ing

They are playing.

💬 Examples

I am learning English.
أنا أتعلم الإنجليزية.

He is watching TV.
هو يشاهد التلفاز.

She is reading a book.
هي تقرأ كتابًا.

We are studying English.
نحن ندرس الإنجليزية.

They are playing football.
هم يلعبون كرة القدم.

❌ Negative

I am not sleeping.

He is not working.

They are not playing.

You can also use:

is not = isn't
are not = aren't

❓ Questions

Are you studying?

Is she reading?

Are they playing football?

Short Answers

Yes, I am.

Yes, she is.

No, they aren't.
