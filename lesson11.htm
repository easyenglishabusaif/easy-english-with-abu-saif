1️⃣1️⃣ Future Simple

المستقبل البسيط

📚 What is Future Simple?

نستخدم المستقبل البسيط للتحدث عن أفعال أو أحداث ستحدث في المستقبل.

We use the Future Simple to talk about actions or events that will happen in the future.

💡 Basic Rule

Subject + will + base verb

جميع الضمائر تستخدم: will

I / You / He / She / It / We / They → will

✍️ Examples

I will study English tomorrow.

She will visit London next week.

He will finish his homework tonight.

We will watch a movie tomorrow.

They will learn English very well.

⏰ Future Time Expressions

نستخدم كلمات معينة للإشارة إلى المستقبل.

tomorrow = غدًا

next week = الأسبوع القادم

next month = الشهر القادم

next year = السنة القادمة

tonight = الليلة

⏳ Remember

Future Simple يستخدم للتحدث عن شيء سيحدث في المستقبل.

I will study English tomorrow.

أولًا: نحن الآن في الحاضر.

ثانيًا: سأدرس الإنجليزية غدًا.

❌ Negative

نستخدم: will not + base verb

will not = won't

I won't study tomorrow.

She won't visit London.

They won't watch the movie.

He won't finish his homework tonight.

❓ Questions

نستخدم: Will + subject + base verb?

Will you study tomorrow?

Will she visit London?

Will they watch the movie?

Will he finish his homework?

💬 Short Answers

Yes, I will.

No, I won't.

Yes, she will.

No, she won't.

⭐ Important Words

will = سوف

I will study tomorrow.

tomorrow = غدًا

She will visit London tomorrow.

next = القادم

We will travel next week.

tonight = الليلة

He will study tonight.

🔄 Will vs Will Not

Positive

She will visit London.

أما النفي:

She will not visit London.

ويمكن اختصارها:

She won't visit London.

🔄 Present vs Future

Present Simple

I study English every day.

الفعل يحدث بشكل معتاد في الحاضر.


Future Simple

I will study English tomorrow.

الفعل سيحدث في المستقبل.
