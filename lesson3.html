3️⃣ Present Simple

نستخدم المضارع البسيط للتحدث عن العادات والروتين والحقائق.

💡 Basic Rule

I / You / We / They → verb

He / She / It → verb + s

Subject Verb Example المعنى
I play I play football. أنا ألعب كرة القدم.
You play You play football. أنت تلعب كرة القدم.
We play We play football. نحن نلعب كرة القدم.
They play They play football. هم يلعبون كرة القدم.
He plays He plays football. هو يلعب كرة القدم.
She plays She plays football. هي تلعب كرة القدم.
It works It works well. إنه يعمل جيدًا.

✍️ Examples

I play football.

She plays tennis.

They work every day.

He likes coffee.

We study English.

❌ Negative

I don't play football.

You don't like coffee.

They don't work here.

He doesn't play football.

She doesn't like coffee.

❓ Questions

Do you play football?

Do they work here?

Does he play football?

Does she like coffee?

⭐ Remember

بعد does / doesn't نستخدم الفعل بدون s.

Does he play football? ✅

Does he plays football? ❌
