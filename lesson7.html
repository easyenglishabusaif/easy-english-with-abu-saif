7️⃣ Present Perfect

المضارع التام

📚 What is Present Perfect?

نستخدم المضارع التام للتحدث عن حدث وقع في الماضي وله علاقة بالحاضر.

We use the Present Perfect for actions that happened in the past and are connected to the present.

🧩 The Formula

Subject + have / has + V3

I / You / We / They → have

He / She / It → has

💬 Examples

I have finished my homework.

She has visited London.

He has eaten breakfast.

We have watched this movie.

They have cleaned the room.

⭐ What is V3?

V3 is the past participle, which is the third form of the verb.

go → went → gone

eat → ate → eaten

see → saw → seen

play → played → played

work → worked → worked

❌ Negative

We use: have not / has not

have not = haven't

has not = hasn't

I haven't finished my homework.

She hasn't visited London.

They haven't eaten yet.

❓ Questions

Have you finished your homework?

Has she visited London?

Have they eaten lunch?

Short Answers

Yes, I have.

No, I haven't.

Yes, she has.

No, she hasn't.

⭐ Important Words

already = بالفعل

I have already finished.

just = للتو

She has just arrived.

yet = بعد / حتى الآن

I haven't finished yet.

ever = من قبل

Have you ever visited London?

never = أبدًا

I have never been to London.

🔄 Past Simple vs Present Perfect

Past Simple: نستخدمه عندما نحدد وقتًا انتهى في الماضي.

I visited London last year.

Present Perfect: لا نحدد وقتًا منتهيًا، ونركز على النتيجة أو الخبرة.

I have visited London.

لاحظ الفرق:

I visited London last year. ✅

I have visited London. ✅
