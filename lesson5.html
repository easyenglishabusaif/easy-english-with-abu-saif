5️⃣ Past Simple

الماضي البسيط

📚 What is Past Simple?

We use the Past Simple to talk about actions that happened in the past.

نستخدم الماضي البسيط للتحدث عن أفعال حدثت في الماضي.

Examples:

I visited my friend yesterday.

She watched TV last night.

🧩 Regular Verbs

Many regular verbs form the past by adding -ed.

play → played

work → worked

watch → watched

visit → visited

💬 Examples

I played football yesterday.

She watched a movie last night.

We visited our friend.

They worked yesterday.

⭐ Irregular Verbs

Some verbs have a special past form.

go → went

eat → ate

see → saw

come → came

have → had

drink → drank

❌ Negative

We use did not or didn't.

I didn't play football.

She didn't watch TV.

They didn't go to school.

Remember: after didn't, use the base verb.

I didn't play. ✅

I didn't played. ❌

❓ Questions

We use Did to ask questions.

Did you play football?

Did she watch TV?

Did they go to school?

Short Answers

Yes, I did.

No, I didn't.

⏰ Time Expressions

yesterday = أمس

last night = الليلة الماضية

last week = الأسبوع الماضي

last year = السنة الماضية

two days ago = منذ يومين
